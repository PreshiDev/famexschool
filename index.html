About Our School

Preparing Students for Success

Famex International School is committed to providing quality education that equips students with the knowledge, skills, and values needed to thrive in a rapidly changing world.

Located at 20, Harmony Street, Oke Ola, Off Itele Road, Ota, Ogun State, our school offers a nurturing environment where students can explore their potential and develop into well-rounded individuals.

Our dedicated team of well-educated and qualified teachers ensures that each student receives personalized attention and guidance to achieve academic excellence.

Qualified Teachers

Our teaching staff consists of highly qualified and experienced educators dedicated to student success.
